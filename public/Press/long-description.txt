You are Hardluck Wu — drowning in debt, unemployed, hunted by collectors. With nowhere left to turn, you arrive at DULAN, a legendary underground casino where every victory buys another chance to survive, and every loss might cost you everything.

DULAN is built around a simple question: could a traditional card game carry an entire story? Rather than reinventing Big Two (Chinese Poker) with roguelike mechanics or deckbuilding systems, this game keeps the familiar rules exactly as they are — and wraps a complete narrative adventure around them.

distinct opponents stand between you and the truth behind this casino, each with a fully realized personality: a gluttonous fool who can't stop talking about food, a gaudy nouveau-riche showoff, a venomous schemer hiding behind a sweet smile, a patient old fox who speaks only in proverbs. Every opponent has their own room, their own soundtrack, and dialogue that reacts throughout the match — taunts, impatience, and reactions to how close you are to winning or losing.

One-time-use skill cards — peek at your opponent's hand, swap a card, or force them to pass — can turn a losing hand around. But how you win matters just as much as whether you win: three distinct endings await, including a hidden True Ending reserved for those who finish the game without ever relying on a skill.

DULAN was designed, written, and programmed solo by a Taiwan-based developer, with AI-assisted art and music (fully disclosed on the store page). It supports English, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese, with full keyboard, mouse, and controller support.
