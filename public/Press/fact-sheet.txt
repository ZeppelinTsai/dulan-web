DULAN (賭爛) — Fact Sheet

Title: DULAN (賭爛)
Genre: Story-driven card game / narrative adventure
Engine: Phaser 3 + Vite + TypeScript (packaged for Windows via Electron)
Platform: Windows (itch.io)
Price: US$3.99
Languages: English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese
Release Date: August 4, 2026
Developer: ZeppelinTsai (solo, Taiwan-based)
Playtime: Around 10–30 minutes per playthrough

Store Page: https://zeppelintsai.itch.io/dulan
Gameplay Video: https://youtu.be/OlMKSe15ncg

About the Developer
ZeppelinTsai is a solo indie developer based in Taiwan. DULAN is their first commercial release — designed, written, and programmed from the ground up, with AI-assisted art and music (fully disclosed on the store page).

Key Features
- Story-driven Big Two campaign — original rules kept intact, not a roguelike or deckbuilder
- Optional Big Two tutorial for first-time players
- Short and replayable — around 10–30 minutes per playthrough
- Unique opponents, each with their own personality, room, soundtrack, and dialogue
- One-time-use skill cards that can turn a losing hand around
- Three endings, including a hidden True Ending
- Unlockable character, CG, and music gallery
- 5 manual save slots plus auto-save
- Full keyboard and mouse support
